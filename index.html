🎬 Film Contacts Extractor

Reads your Zoho call sheet emails and builds a master contacts spreadsheet — by Tom Osborn

? What does this tool do?

It searches your Zoho email inbox for all your call sheet emails, reads through each one, and pulls out every crew member's name, role, phone number and email address. At the end it creates a tidy .xlsx spreadsheet — one row per person, with every shoot they appeared on.

It does this automatically. You just click Start and leave it running.

1 Before you start — one-time setup

This tool needs to be able to log into your Zoho email and your Claude account. Both connections are already built in. There is nothing to install.

⚠️ Important: Open this file in Google Chrome or Firefox. It will not work properly in Internet Explorer or older browsers.

2 How to run it

  1. Click the big Start the extractor button below
  2. Click Start Fresh on the next screen
  3. Leave it running — it will work through your emails automatically
  4. Every few emails, a file called filmcontacts_checkpoint.json will save itself to your Downloads folder — this is your progress backup, keep it safe
  5. When it finishes, click Download spreadsheet
✅ You don't need to watch it. Feel free to do other things while it runs — just don't close this browser tab.

3 If it stops before finishing

Because this tool makes a lot of requests to Claude, it may hit a usage limit part-way through. Don't worry — it saves your progress automatically.

  1. When it stops, your progress is already saved in filmcontacts_checkpoint.json in your Downloads folder
  2. Wait a few hours for your Claude usage to refresh (or come back the next day)
  3. Open this file again in your browser
  4. Click Start the extractor again
  5. Scroll down to Step 4 on the next screen
  6. Drag the filmcontacts_checkpoint.json file from your Downloads folder and drop it into the box
  7. Click Resume — it will pick up exactly where it left off
⚠️ For roughly 380 emails, you may need to do this 2–3 times in total. Each session takes 30–60 minutes.

4 The spreadsheet you'll get

The file FilmContacts_Master.xlsx will have four sheets:

  • Crew Contacts — one row per crew person, with all their shoots listed across
  • Agency & Production — same layout for agency and production company contacts
  • Productions — one row per production
  • All Shoots — every shoot day as its own row

Grey italic text means the tool wasn't 100% certain about that piece of information. Location cells have a clickable Maps link.
